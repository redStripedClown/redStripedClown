## 🌷 About me

I'm a student at the **Federal Institute of Paraná (IFPR)**, currently completing a technical program in **Systems Development** integrated with high school.

I'm interested in software development, systems analysis, applied research, assistive technology, and the intersection between software and hardware. I enjoy building practical solutions, exploring new technologies, and collaborating on projects that bring ideas to life.

> *The name's a circus act; the work is accessibility engineering. Both are intentional.*
